INBOX TRIAGE: the questions sent to Jev
One call per email. Four questions, evaluated in parallel. State = the email (from, subject, date, body).
These definitions are also the ground truth definitions used to generate the dataset (generation/SPEC.txt).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. spam (noul)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Question: Is this message unsolicited?

Criteria
true:  the recipient never asked for this message and has no relationship with the sender:
       cold sales outreach, mass marketing without a subscription, phishing, scams,
       fake invoices, SEO or link-building offers, recruitment blasts.
false: the message comes from an existing relationship or from something the recipient
       signed up for: members, visitors asking about the space, partners, suppliers,
       newsletters the recipient subscribed to, notifications from services in use
       (event platform, bank, parcel delivery, booking tools), automatic replies.

Note: a newsletter the recipient subscribed to is NOT spam, even when it is pure marketing.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. category (choice)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Question: What is this message mainly about?

Options
membership      joining the space, prices, trial day, desk availability, leaving
event           hosting or proposing an event, meetup, workshop, AV or room setup for an event
room_booking    booking, changing or cancelling a meeting room or a desk for a day
partnership     sponsoring, co-branding, press, a collaboration proposal from an organisation
complaint       something is wrong: noise, wifi, access card, heating, cleaning, a bad experience
billing         invoices, payments, receipts, VAT numbers, failed card
newsletter      a subscribed newsletter or an automatic notification from a service in use
spam            unsolicited commercial, fraudulent or irrelevant message (see question 1)
other           anything else: thanks, lost and found, a question that fits no option

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. urgency (score, 4 levels)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Question: How soon does this message need action?

Levels (each described on its own, not relative to the others)
1  no action needed: a newsletter, a notification, a thank-you note, spam
2  answer within the week: a general question, a proposal for next month, a request with no date
3  answer within 24 hours: a request for this week, a booking change, a payment question
4  answer today: something happening today or tomorrow, a person locked out, a safety or
   security issue, a payment that is failing right now

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. needs_human (noul)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Question: Does this message need a reply written by a person?

Criteria
true:  the sender asks something, requests something, or reports a problem, and expects a reply.
false: nothing to reply to: newsletters, notifications, automatic replies, spam, pure FYI.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
How the demo uses the answers (in code, not in Jev)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- spam above 0.7: archive. Below 0.3: inbox. Between: "check" column.
- needs_human above 0.5 and urgency 3 or 4: top of the list.
- The benchmark (Sonnet vs Jev vs the 25-lines script) uses question 1 only, as a binary task.
- Category accuracy is measured on non-spam emails only.
