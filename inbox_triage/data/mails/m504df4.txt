Subject: Harbour District Forum: Autumn Bulletin
Date: 2026-10-05 09:00

Dear subscribers,

The Harbour District Business Forum is pleased to present its Autumn Bulletin. You are receiving this because your organisation registered for our bulletin in March 2026.

1. Annual Members' Assembly
The Assembly will be held on 12 November at 17:00 at Pakhus Hall. The agenda and the proposed budget are attached to the digital edition. Registration is encouraged, though not required, and the fee for non-members is DKK 250.

2. Quayside lighting works
Works on the quayside lighting between Bridge 3 and Bridge 5 will start on 19 October and last approximately four weeks. Pedestrian access will be maintained at all times.

3. Call for proposals: the Winter Market
Local businesses and shared workspaces may submit stall proposals until 30 October using the form on our site. Selected participants receive a subsidised stall.

4. Sponsorship opportunities
Our partners programme offers visibility in the bulletin and at the Assembly. A rate card is available on request.

5. A word of thanks
The Forum thanks all the organisations that opened their doors during the Harbour Open Week in September. More than 600 visitors attended. No reply to this message is necessary.

6. Next bulletin
The December issue will cover the results of the member survey and the calendar for 2027.

Yours faithfully,
The Secretariat
