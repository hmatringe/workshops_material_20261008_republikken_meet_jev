Hello and welcome to the October edition of The Coworking Digest, the monthly round-up you signed up for.

THIS MONTH IN SHARED WORKSPACES

1. Quiet hours are back. Several spaces in northern Europe are trying out two silent blocks per day, from 9 to 11 and from 14 to 16. Early reports suggest members like it, and phone booth queues get shorter.

2. Pricing trends. A survey of 140 flexible workspaces shows desk prices rising about 4 percent over the last year, while meeting room hire has stayed flat.

3. Community managers speak. We interviewed three managers about what keeps members coming back. The common answer: regular small events beat big occasional ones.
