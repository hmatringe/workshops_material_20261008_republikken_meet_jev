Welcome to the October edition of The Desk, the monthly letter from the Lykkegade community. You are receiving it because you signed up at the front desk or on our site.

THIS MONTH AT A GLANCE

1. Two new meeting rooms are open
The old storage room on the second floor is now Room Pelikan (4 seats) and Room Tangle (8 seats). Both have screens and whiteboards. Book them through the usual booking tool.

2. Autumn offer: 20% off day passes
Bring a friend and your next three day passes cost 20% less. The code is HYGGE20 and works until 31 October. This is a limited offer, so grab it while it lasts.

3. Chair auction on Friday 16 October
We are replacing 30 old chairs. Bids start at DKK 50, proceeds go to the community kitchen fund. Doors open at 16:00.

4. Member spotlight
This month we meet Aiko from Paperbridge, a two-person studio that makes tactile maps. She has worked from desk 9 since March and gives her tip on surviving a Danish winter: wool socks and a thermos.

5. Quiet hours reminder
The third floor remains a quiet zone until 14:00 on weekdays. Phone calls belong in the booths.

6. Coming up
Founders' breakfast, 8 October. Open lab on paper prototyping, 21 October. Halloween potluck, 30 October.

That's all for now. See you in the kitchen.
