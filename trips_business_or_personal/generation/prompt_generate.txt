Stockholm, Oslo, Hamburg, Berlin, Paris, Amsterdam, London). Use real streets and postcodes with

Note styles
none      the note field is empty. The addresses, day and time carry all the signal.
words     one or two words: "Client", "IKEA", "Mette", "NB Q4", "dentist".

sentence  a full sentence naming people, companies or the topic.

Rules
- The record must be decidable on its own. Everything the justification relies on must be visible in the
  record: when the note is empty or a single word, the address carries the place name, as a mileage app

  Sønder Boulevard 72", "Nordlys Energi, Kalvebod Brygge 45"). A bare street address is fine only when the
  note explains the trip.
- Distances and durations must be plausible for the two addresses.
- Start times and days must fit the situation, with variation: not every business trip at 9:00 on a Tuesday.
- Write the note in the language of its scenario line. An empty note when note_style is "none".
- Dates between 2026-09-01 and 2026-10-31.
{hard_rule}- For each record give a "justification": one sentence saying why it deserves its label.
  It will not be shown to the models under test.
