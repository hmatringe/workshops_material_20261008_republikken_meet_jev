You write examples for an evaluation dataset of trip classifiers (business or personal), the kind of
records a mileage-tracking app stores for a driver.
You must produce exactly 10 trip records. Each record follows one scenario line below, in order.
The label in the scenario line is the instruction, not a guess: the record must clearly deserve it.

Context
The driver works in Copenhagen for a small company and sometimes travels. Trips are by car, taxi or
rental car, in and around Copenhagen and in cities within three hours of flight (Malmö, Aarhus, Odense,
Stockholm, Oslo, Hamburg, Berlin, Paris, Amsterdam, London). Use real streets and postcodes with
invented house numbers. Invent every company and person. The driver's home and usual office are
up to you but must stay consistent within the batch.

Definitions
TRIPS: the question sent to Jev
One call per batch of trips, one noul question per trip. State = the trip record (date, start time, duration,
distance, start address, end address, the driver's note) plus two fields computed in code: weekday and
"within working hours". Dates and arithmetic stay in code: Jev reads them literally.
These definitions are also the ground truth definitions used to generate the dataset (generation/SPEC.txt).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
business (noul)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Question: Is this a business trip?

Criteria
true:  the main purpose of the trip is work: meeting a client, a prospect, a supplier or a partner;
       moving between two work sites; a conference, a trade fair or a training attended for work;
       a site or store visit; driving to or from the airport or station for a work trip; picking up
       a visiting client; a business meal; an errand done for the company (bank, post, customs);
       a rental car used between a hotel and a client abroad. Evenings and weekends do not matter:
       a client dinner on a Saturday is business.
false: the main purpose is private: home, family, school, groceries, doctor, sport, friends, holidays,
       restaurants, shopping, a partner's workplace. Commuting between home and the usual workplace
       is personal, as in the mileage rules of Denmark and most countries. A trip whose main purpose
       is private stays personal even if it passes by the office or ends near a client.

Where a human hesitates (the hard cases)
- Home to the usual office: personal (commute). Home straight to a client: business.
- A weekend or evening trip to a restaurant: business only if the record shows a client or a work purpose.
- Airport trips: business or personal depending on the purpose of the flight, not the airport.
- A trip to a café, a colleague's home or a hotel: the note or the context decides.
- Lunch alone during work hours: personal. Lunch with a client: business.

Difficulty for this batch: hard
Misleading signals of the opposite class, but an attentive reader justifies the label in one sentence. NOT truly ambiguous.
Hard business: Saturday client dinner; Sunday airport pickup of a visiting client; café with a prospect; a colleague's home
for a work session; home straight to a client at 8:30 (looks like a commute); a rental car abroad with a hotel as start.
Hard personal: home to the usual office (commute); lunch alone near the office at 12:30; dropping a partner at their
workplace; a trade fair visited on a day off; the gym next to the office; a weekend trip to Malmö with friends; an
airport run for a holiday.

Scenario lines (one record each, in this order)
1. business=false purpose=partner_dropoff language=da note_style=sentence | dropping a partner or a friend at their workplace or station
2. business=false purpose=lunch_alone language=en note_style=words | lunch alone during work hours
3. business=false purpose=commute language=fr note_style=none | home to the usual workplace or back, weekday, rush hour
4. business=false purpose=sport language=en note_style=short | gym, swimming, football, running club
5. business=false purpose=leisure_trip language=da note_style=none | a weekend away, a summerhouse, a museum, a concert, a beach
6. business=false purpose=airport_holiday language=en note_style=short | airport or station for a holiday or a private visit, or the return
7. business=false purpose=restaurant_private language=en note_style=short | a restaurant or café without any work purpose
8. business=false purpose=partner_dropoff language=en note_style=none | dropping a partner or a friend at their workplace or station
9. business=false purpose=lunch_alone language=fr note_style=sentence | lunch alone during work hours
10. business=false purpose=commute language=en note_style=words | home to the usual workplace or back, weekday, rush hour

Each scenario line gives: business (true or false), purpose, language of the note (en, da or fr),
note_style, and the situation.

Note styles
none      the note field is empty. The addresses, day and time carry all the signal.
words     one or two words: "Client", "IKEA", "Mette", "NB Q4", "dentist".
short     a short note, may use abbreviations or a typo: "mtg w/ Nordlys re Q4 offer".
sentence  a full sentence naming people, companies or the topic.

Rules
- The record must be decidable on its own. Everything the justification relies on must be visible in the
  record: when the note is empty or a single word, the address carries the place name, as a mileage app
  shows it from the map ("Fysioterapi Vesterbro, Vesterbrogade 120, 1620 København V", "Café Dyrehaven,
  Sønder Boulevard 72", "Nordlys Energi, Kalvebod Brygge 45"). A bare street address is fine only when the
  note explains the trip.
- Distances and durations must be plausible for the two addresses.
- Start times and days must fit the situation, with variation: not every business trip at 9:00 on a Tuesday.
- Write the note in the language of its scenario line. An empty note when note_style is "none".
- Dates between 2026-09-01 and 2026-10-31.
- Hard batch: the misleading signal must sit in the record itself (the day, the hour, the kind of place, the wording
  of the note), and the deciding detail must still be there for an attentive reader.
- For each record give a "justification": one sentence saying why it deserves its label.
  It will not be shown to the models under test.
